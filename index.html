PILOT PROGRAM

Free Financial Health Check for Coffee Shops & Wellness Businesses

We're launching a pilot program specifically for Dubai's coffee shops, cafes, and wellness centers. These businesses face unique financial challenges — unpredictable cash flow, hidden cost leaks, and labor inefficiencies. We're offering a complimentary Financial Health Check to help identify where money is being lost and how to fix it.

Cash Flow Volatility

45%

of cafés and wellness centers struggle with unpredictable cash flow. Revenue fluctuates weekly while fixed costs like rent and salaries remain constant, creating cash crunches that force reactive decisions.

Food Cost Leakage

35-40%

Most cafés run food costs between 35-40% when the industry benchmark is 28-32%. That 7-10% gap translates to AED 10,000-15,000 walking out the door monthly — invisible until measured.

Labor Inefficiency

30%+

Without data, owners schedule by habit — overstaffing during slow hours, understaffing during peaks. Labor should be 30-35% of revenue, but without tracking, optimization is impossible.

What's Included in Your Free Financial Health Check

  • Cash Flow Gap Analysis: We map your average monthly cash position and identify which weeks run tight, with specific recommendations for smoothing volatility
  • Food Cost Reality Check: Your actual food cost percentage vs. the 28-32% benchmark, with estimated annual cost of the gap and immediate optimization opportunities
  • Labor Efficiency Report: Your labor cost percentage vs. 30-35% benchmark, with annual cost impact and actionable staffing recommendations
  • One-Page Action Plan: Three specific, ranked recommendations with estimated monthly savings for immediate implementation
